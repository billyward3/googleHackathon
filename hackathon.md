## Overview

Communities depend on complex systems (transportation, sustainability, government services, urban planning) that are often inefficient and inaccessible. Build solutions leveraging technology as a catalyst for efficiency. Open to engineering, design, data, and policy perspectives.

**Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI):** Foundational digital systems supporting public services at scale -- digital identity, open data platforms, public APIs, payment/benefits systems, civic dashboards.

**Goal:** Build a **minimum viable product** in one day. Judged on scope, feasibility, and impact (must have a technical implementation plan).

**Deliverables can include:** Web/mobile apps, data dashboards, prototypes/MVPs, Figma boards, slideshow presentations.

## Sub-Tracks

### 1. Mobility & Transportation

**Problem:** Fragmented, unreliable urban transit. Delays, inconsistent scheduling, limited real-time info. Coverage gaps in underserved/low-income areas. Barriers for disabled, vehicle-less, and transit-desert residents limiting access to jobs, education, services.

**Focus:** Real-time tracking/prediction, route optimization, first/last-mile connectivity, accessibility (affordability, disability, safety)

### 2. Resource Allocation & Sustainability

**Problem:** Cities allocate energy/water/waste inefficiently due to slow bureaucratic processes. Lack of real-time data, transparency, and community input creates misaligned distribution.

**Focus:** Energy efficiency, waste management, water distribution, sustainable urban incentives

### 3. Government Services & Access

**Problem:** Public services are confusing, time-consuming, and inaccessible -- especially for underserved populations. Scattered info, complex language, unclear eligibility, language barriers, limited digital literacy.

**Focus:** Benefits access, government UX, transparency/trust, digital identity/verification

### 4. Public Disaster & Emergency Response

**Problem:** Delayed, fragmented, inaccessible information during crises. Poor cross-sector coordination, inadequate real-time resource allocation. Vulnerable populations disproportionately affected.

**Focus:** Emergency communication, disaster coordination, crisis resource allocation, public safety alerts

### 5. Housing & Urban Development (OUR TRACK)

**Problem:** Rising costs, limited supply, gentrification, unequal development. Zoning/land-use decisions are complex and opaque. Tenants struggle with legal protections, support services, eviction/displacement.

**Focus:** Housing affordability/availability, zoning transparency, community development, tenant resources/protections

**Prompts:**
- How might we improve access to affordable housing?
- How might we make urban planning decisions more transparent?
- How might we support tenants in navigating housing systems?
